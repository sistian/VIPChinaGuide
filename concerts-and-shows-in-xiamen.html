🎭 Concerts and Shows in Xiamen

Xiamen's coastal location and colonial heritage have created a rich performance culture — from the world-renowned Gulangyu Piano Museum concerts to the annual International Maritime Tourism Festival.

🎵 Top Performances in Xiamen

🎹

Gulangyu Piano Museum Concerts

Gulangyu is famous worldwide for its piano culture — the Piano Museum hosts regular performances by visiting international pianists. The island itself has regular street piano performances in the evenings.

🎭

Xiamen International Performing Arts Festival

Held annually (usually October/November), this multi-week festival brings theater, dance, opera, and musical performances from around the world to Xiamen's grand theaters.

🐉

Xiamen Dragon Boat Festival Racing

The annual Dragon Boat Festival races (usually June) at Xiamen's coastal waters — teams compete to the beat of traditional drums, with cultural performances along the shore.

🎸

Xiamen Rock and Indie Music Scene

The coastal city's university area has a small but dedicated indie rock scene — venues near Xiamen University host local bands and touring acts. Small cover charges.

🎻

Xiamen Philharmonic Performances

Regular performances by the Xiamen Philharmonic Orchestra at the Xiamen Cultural Centre — classical repertoire, film scores, and Chinese compositions.

🌊

Hulang (Hooray) Coastal Music Events

Seasonal outdoor music events at the coastal area near Huangcuo Beach — summer electronic music, jazz evenings, and sunset DJ sets.
