Sports Events in Zhangjiajie

Major sporting events in Zhangjiajie - from world-class marathons to international tournaments. Complete guide with past results and future outlook.

Top Sports Events in Zhangjiajie

🏃

Zhangjiajie International Marathon (张家界马拉松)

Marathon and half marathon through UNESCO Wulingyuan scenic area. Course passes dramatic quartz-sandstone pillar formations that inspired Avatar. One of China's most visually spectacular marathon venues. Usually October.

Full name: Zhangjiajie International Marathon (张家界马拉松)

🏔️

Zhangjiajie Mountain Challenge Race

Ultra trail running through Wulingyuan UNESCO site. Challenging terrain through the pillar formations, forests, and gorges. Multiple distance categories including 100km ultra. Usually October.

Full name: Zhangjiajie Mountain Challenge (张家界山体挑战赛)

🏃

Tianmen Mountain Skyway Marathon (天门山通天大道马拉松)

Marathon on the famous Tianmen Mountain sky road (天门山通天大道). The 99-bend road to Tianmen Cave is one of world's most challenging road running courses. Usually September.

Full name: Tianmen Mountain Skyway Marathon (天门山马拉松)

🥾

Wulingyuan Trail Running Festival

Trail running through UNESCO Wulingyuan scenic area. Multiple distance options. Participants run past the pillar formations that inspired Avatar's floating mountains. Usually October.

Full name: Wulingyuan Trail Running Festival (武陵源越野节)

🏃

Zhangjiajie University Mountain Race

University athletics event in the Wulingyuan area. Regional university athletes compete in track and mountain running events. Usually May.

Full name: Zhangjiajie University Mountain Race (张家界大学山地赛)

🚣

Furong Lake Dragon Boat Race

Dragon boat racing at Furong Lake near Zhangjiajie. Traditional boat races with local teams competing. Cultural event celebrating Dragon Boat Festival. Usually June.

Full name: Furong Lake Dragon Boat Race (芙蓉湖龙舟赛)

Past Results and Future Outlook

Looking back at recent years and what to expect in 2026-2027

2024-2025

Recent Highlights

Zhangjiajie Marathon 2025 was the largest edition with 20,000 runners. The Tianmen Mountain Skyway Marathon had its first international winner in 2025. The Wulingyuan Ultra was recognized as one of China's most scenic trail events.

2026

2026 Season Outlook

Zhangjiajie Marathon 2026 aims to attract 25,000 runners. The Wulingyuan trail event is expanding with new routes through the canyon areas. New events are being planned for the Fenghuang ancient town nearby.

2027

2027 and Beyond

Zhangjiajie is establishing itself as China's most scenic sports events destination. New ultra-trail events are planned for the UNESCO site area. The city is developing additional mountain running routes in the surrounding national park.
