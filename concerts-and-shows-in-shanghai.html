🎭 Concerts and Shows in Shanghai

hanghai is China's entertainment capital — from the Shanghai Opera House to underground jazz bars and massive arena concerts. Here's where to find the best performances.

🎵 Top Performances in Shanghai

🎭

Shanghai Opera House Performances (上海大剧院)

he Shanghai Opera House is the city's premier performing arts venue — Shanghai Philharmonic concerts, ballet, Chinese opera, and Broadway touring productions. Book through the official website or your guide.

🎷

Jz Club Shanghai — World-Class Jazz

Shanghai's most famous jazz venue — Jz Club hosts the best jazz musicians in Asia with nightly live performances. Also has an annual Jz Festival (usually November). Covers from CNY 100-300.

🎸

Shanghai International Music Festival

Held annually (usually October) — classical music, jazz, and world music performances across the city's major concert halls. International orchestras and soloists headline.

🎤

Mercedes-Benz Arena Shanghai Concerts

Shanghai's largest arena — major international pop stars, K-pop groups, and Chinese A-list celebrities perform here. Check local ticketing platforms (Mai Mai, Damai) for upcoming shows.

🏛️

Shanghai International Film Festival Screenings

Held annually in June — film screenings, red carpet premieres, and director talks at venues across the city. One of Asia's most prestigious film festivals.

🎹

Shanghai Symphony Hall Performances

One of China's finest concert halls — the Shanghai Symphony Orchestra performs here with international guest conductors. Also hosts chamber music and solo recitals. Acoustics rival the best in Asia.
