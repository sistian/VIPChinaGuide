How to Get to Qingdao ✈️

How to get to Qingdao — metro (7 lines), taxi, DiDi, Qingdao & Qingdao North train stations, Tsingtao Brewery transport, long-distance bus. Complete 2024 guide.

🚆 Qingdao Transport Options

🚇

Qingdao Metro — 7 Lines

Get 青岛通 card. Official app: 青岛地铁. Key: Line 3 (to Badaguan/Zhoushan), Line 2 (to Qingdao North).

Qingdao Taxi Fares

Starts CNY 10 for 3 km, CNY 2/km. Hills and coastal roads make short rides scenic. All metered.

DiDi Ride-Hailing (滴滴出行)

Widely available. For Lao Shan Mountain, taxi recommended.

Qingdao Train Stations

Qingdao Station (青岛站) — beautiful German colonial station by the sea: classic trains. Qingdao North (青岛北站) — HSR hub: Beijing (3.5h), Shanghai (5h), Jinan (2h).

Qingdao Long-Distance Bus

Qingdao Bus Station (青岛汽车站) near railway station. Routes to Jinan, Yantai, Weihai.
