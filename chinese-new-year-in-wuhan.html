CNY 2027 Activities in Wuhan

When is Chinese New Year 2027?

hinese New Year 2027 is February 17, 2027. The Chinese calendar (农历) is a lunisolar calendar - CNY falls between January 21 and February 20. CNY 2027 is the Year of the Sheep/Goat (Geng Shen). The Chinese Spring Festival (春节) traditionally lasts 15 days, ending with Lantern Festival (元宵节).

CNY Eve Reunion Dinner

The most important evening - families gather for reunion dinner (年夜饭). In Wuhan, locals enjoy traditional foods and watch midnight fireworks to welcome the new year.

Temple Fairs and Markets

Traditional temple fairs (庙会) pop up across Wuhan during the Spring Festival. Visitors enjoy folk performances, lantern displays, traditional foods, and cultural activities.

CNY Foods in Wuhan

Traditional Chinese New Year foods include dumplings (饺子 Jiaozi) for wealth, whole fish (鱼 Yu) for abundance, and nian gao (年糕) rice cake for progress. The Chinese Spring Festival food traditions vary by region.

Lantern Festival (元宵节)

The 15th day of CNY - Lantern Festival (元宵节) marks the end of the Spring Festival. In Wuhan, special lantern displays and tangyuan (glutinous rice balls) celebrations take place.

CNY Facts - Chinese Spring Festival

Chinese New Year 2027 Date

Chinese New Year 2027 is February 17, 2027. Also called Chinese Spring Festival (春节) or Lunar New Year. This date is determined by the Chinese lunar calendar.

Chinese Calendar

The Chinese calendar (农历) is a lunisolar calendar. Each year has a zodiac animal (生肖) and heavenly stem and earthly branch (干支). 2027 is Geng Shen (庚申) - Year of the Metal Goat/Sheep.

The Chinese Zodiac

The Chinese zodiac (十二生肖) cycles through 12 animals: Rat, Ox, Tiger, Rabbit, Dragon, Snake, Horse, Goat, Monkey, Rooster, Dog, Pig. The Goat is the 8th animal.

Chinese Spring Festival Food

Chinese Spring Festival food traditions: Jiaozi dumplings (饺子) shaped like gold ingots for wealth; whole fish (鱼) for abundance; longevity noodles (长寿面) for long life; nian gao (年糕) rice cake for progress.
