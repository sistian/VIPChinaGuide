🎭 Concerts and Shows in Dali

ali's nightlife and arts scene is one of the most vibrant in Yunnan — from intimate live music in courtyard bars to annual music festivals under the Cangshan mountains.

🎵 Top Performances in Dali

🎵

Dali Old Town Live Music Bars

he narrow streets of the old town are filled with small live music venues every evening — local Yunnan bands, folk musicians, and traveling artists perform in courtyard bars. Cover charge usually CNY 20-50. Best explored with a local guide who knows tonight's lineup.

🎸

Dali International Music Festival

Held annually in Dali (usually April/May), this multi-day outdoor festival attracts musicians from across China and Southeast Asia. Past lineups have included indie rock, folk, and electronic acts. Tickets sell out quickly.

🎭

Dali Bai Minority Cultural Performance

Evening cultural shows presenting traditional Bai dances, music, and ceremonies. Performed by local Bai artists in the old town's main theater. A window into Yunnan's rich ethnic heritage.

🎤

Catherine's Bar — Legendary Open Mic

Dali's most famous bar, Catherine's has been running open mic nights for over 30 years. Musicians of all levels perform for an appreciative international crowd. The bonfire nights are legendary.

🌙

Erhai Lake Moonrise Cultural Events

Seasonal evening events at Erhai Lake — traditional Bai music performances on the shore as the moon rises over the water. Organized by local cultural groups, usually in summer and autumn.

🎻

Dali University Arts Centre Performances

The Dali University Arts Centre hosts classical Chinese music, traditional instrument performances, and theater productions throughout the year. Open to visitors. Check with your guide for the current schedule.
