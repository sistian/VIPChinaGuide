How to get to Hangzhou — metro (12 lines), taxi, DiDi, Hangzhou East HSR station, West Lake transport, Longjing tea transport, long-distance bus. Complete 2024 guide.

🚆 Hangzhou Transport Options

🚇

Hangzhou Metro — 12 Lines

Get 杭州通卡 or use Alipay metro QR. Official app: 杭州地铁. Key: Line 1 (East Station), Line 2 (West Lake), Line 7 (West Station).

Hangzhou Taxi Fares

Starts CNY 11 for 3 km, CNY 2.5/km thereafter. West Lake area has many electric taxis.

Hangzhou Train Stations

Hangzhou East (杭州东) — China's busiest HSR: Shanghai (45min), Nanjing (1h), Beijing (4.5h). Hangzhou Station (杭州站) — classic in city center.
