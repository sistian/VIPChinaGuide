📍 Lijiang City Overview

Area

21,219 km²

Lijiang City: 7,600 km² urban, mountainous terrain

Population

1.25 Million

Naxi ethnic minority: ~20% of population

Name Origin

Lijiang (丽江) means 'Beautiful River' — named after the Jinsha River (upper Yangtze) that flows through the region. The Naxi people have lived here for over 1,000 years, creating a unique matriarchal culture and the world's last living pictographic script.

Happiness Index

8.2/10 — Known for its artistic community, laid-back lifestyle, and stunning natural beauty.

Metro & Transport

Airport: Lijiang Sanyi (LJG) — 1 terminal, 40 min to Lijiang Ancient Town by taxi (¥80) or Airport Bus (¥20). Train Stations: Lijiang Railway Station (high-speed rail, 30 min to ancient town), the new railway connecting to Kunming (3.5 hours) and Dali (1.5 hours). Getting Around: Taxis and electric carts; ancient town is pedestrian-only. Bus: Buses to Shangri-La (4 hours) and Tiger Leaping Gorge (2 hours).

Universities

Yunnan University Lijiang Tourism Culture College — specialized in ethnic culture and tourism studies.

Natural Resources

Jade Dragon Snow Mountain (5,596m), Tiger Leaping Gorge (one of the world's deepest), Lugu Lake, and the First Bend of the Yangtze River. A UNESCO World Heritage Site with three parallel rivers.

🍜 Lijiang Food & Shopping Guide

🍽️ Must-Try Foods

  • Lijiang Baba (丽江粑粑) — Crispy wheat flatbread with sweet or savory filling, grilled over charcoal by Naxi street vendors
  • Yunnan Rice Noodles (云南米线) — Crossing-the-bridge style with chicken broth, ham, and fresh vegetables, a Lijiang staple
  • Naxi Grilled Fish (纳西烤鱼) — Fresh trout from the Jade Dragon Snow Mountain streams, grilled with chili and herbs
  • Yak Meat Hotpot (牦牛火锅) — Thinly sliced yak meat cooked in a spicy broth, popular in the colder months
  • Pineapple Rice (菠萝饭) — Glutinous rice steamed inside a hollow pineapple with raisins and coconut
  • Rose Petal Cake (鲜花饼) — Flaky pastry with sweet rose filling, popular souvenir from Lijiang's flower gardens

🛍️ Best Shopping Spots

  • Lijiang Ancient Town (丽江古城) — Naxi silver jewelry, yak wool shawls, and Dongba paper crafts on Xinhua Street
  • Sifang Street (四方街) — The central square market with local snacks, leather goods, and traditional instruments
  • Dongba Paper Shop (东巴纸) — Handmade paper from Naxi 'Dongba' priests, used for ancient pictographic script
  • Lijiang Tea House (茶馆) — Buy Pu'er tea, Tibetan butter tea bricks, and local herbal teas
  • Naxi Embroidery Workshop (纳西刺绣) — Hand-embroidered tapestries and bags with Naxi geometric patterns

📸 Hot Photo Spots

  • Lijiang Ancient Town at night (丽江古城) — Cobblestone streets, red lanterns, and flowing water channels under a starry sky
  • Jade Dragon Snow Mountain (玉龙雪山) — The 5,596m snow-capped peak reflected in Blue Moon Valley, 30 min from the old town
  • Mu's Mansion (木府) — The traditional Naxi palace with intricate wood carvings and mountain views from the upper courtyard
  • Black Dragon Pool (黑龙潭) — The iconic stone bridge with Jade Dragon Snow Mountain reflected in the calm water, a classic postcard view
  • Tiger Leaping Gorge (虎跳峡) — One of the world's deepest gorges, with dramatic cliff views of the roaring Jinsha River

🏛️ Intangible Heritage & Craftsmen

  • Naxi Dongba Pictography (东巴文) — Elder He Jixian of the Naxi Dongba Culture Institute continues to write and teach the world's last living pictographic script, used in Naxi religious ceremonies and 'Dongba' shamanic rituals.
  • Naxi Ancient Music (纳西古乐) — Musician Xuan Ke leads the Naxi Orchestra in performing 500-year-old musical scores preserved on hand-copied manuscripts, known as the 'living fossil of Chinese music'.
  • Lijiang Silver Jewelry (丽江银器) — Naxi silversmith Mu Xingwen creates hand-hammered bracelets and necklaces with Naxi 'flower-in-the-cloud' patterns, a craft passed down through his family for 8 generations.

🎭 Lijiang Concerts & Shows

Lijiang's evenings are filled with traditional Naxi ancient music, minority cultural performances, and live shows set against the backdrop of the UNESCO old town and Jade Dragon Snow Mountain.

🎶

Lijiang Naxi Ancient Music Concert (纳西古乐)

The world-famous Lijiang Naxi Ancient Music Orchestra performs centuries-old melodies that predate the Ming dynasty. Performed nightly in the old town — UNESCO-listed and considered one of China's most precious musical traditions. Book through your guide.

🏔️

印象丽江 (Impression Lijiang) — Open-Air Show

Staged at the foot of Jade Dragon Snow Mountain, this spectacular open-air performance involves hundreds of local Naxi and Yi performers. One of China's most impressive cultural shows. Usually performed March–November weather permitting.

🎭

Lijiang Tinghua Night Performance

Traditional Naxi and Yunnan minority cultural evening performances in the old town's main theater — song, dance, and traditional instrument showcases. Usually start at 8pm nightly.

🎸

Lijiang Old Town Live Music Bars

The old town's bar street has live music venues every night — local bands, indie musicians, and traveling artists. Intimate settings with CNY 20-50 cover charge.

🥁

Naxi Folk Music Heritage Centre Shows

Daily performances of traditional Naxi music by heritage musicians — including the ancient Dongba ritual music passed down through generations. Afternoon and evening shows available.

🎤

Lijiang International Song and Dance Festival

Held annually (usually September), this festival brings together minority song and dance troupes from across Yunnan and Southwest China. Multi-day event with performances across the old town.

🍵 Lijiang Tea Culture Tour

Lijiang is home to some of China's most fascinating tea traditions - from historic tea houses to famous local varieties and time-honored brewing rituals.

🍵

Naxi Pu'er Tea Culture (纳西普洱茶)

Lijiang's Naxi minority has a unique pu'er tea culture - aged pu'er tea served in traditional Naxi copper teapots. Tea houses in the old town serve pu'er from Lijiang's own tea mountains.

🍃

Jade Dragon Snow Mountain Tea (玉龙雪山茶)

High-altitude tea from the Jade Dragon Snow Mountain area - rare, hand-crafted teas from tea gardens at 2,000m elevation. Available at specialty shops in Lijiang old town.

🍵

Lijiang Old Town Tea Houses (古城茶馆)

Traditional Naxi tea houses in the old town - serving local pu'er, mountain herbs, and traditional tea ceremonies. Beautiful wooden buildings in UNESCO heritage settings.

🏔️

Tea Mountains of Lijiang (茶山之旅)

Tea picking tours to the tea mountains near Lijiang - visit Yi and Naxi tea farmers, pick tea leaves, and learn traditional pu'er production methods.

🍵

Lijiang Ancient Tea Horse Road (茶马古道)

The ancient Tea Horse Road (茶马古道) started in Lijiang - explore the historic trade route that carried pu'er tea to Tibet and beyond. Tea culture here dates back centuries.

🍵

Naxi Herbal Tea (纳西草药茶)

Traditional Naxi herbal teas brewed from mountain herbs - unique medicinal tea culture of the Naxi people. Available at local Naxi medicine shops.

Ultra marathon and trail race at the foot of Jade Dragon Snow Mountain (玉龙雪山). One of China's most beautiful mountain marathon venues. Multiple distance options including 100km ultra.

Ultra trail running event through Tiger Leaping Gorge (虎跳峡) - one of world's deepest river canyons. Technical terrain along vertigo-inducing cliffs above the Yangtze River.

Lijiang Old Town & Jade Dragon Snow Mountain

Most Popular 1 Day

Wander through a UNESCO World Heritage ancient town built entirely without walls, then ascend to a 5,596-meter sacred mountain revered by the Naxi people.

Lijiang Old Town (Dayan)

A 1,000-year-old town built by the Naxi ethnic minority using a unique water-supply system where mountain springs flow through every street. Your guide navigates you through the maze of stone-paved lanes past wooden houses with upturned eaves, explains the Dongba pictographic script — the world's last living hieroglyphic writing system with 1,400 characters, and points out the Xuanhe Bridge where Naxi elders still play 1,000-year-old board games.

Jade Dragon Snow Mountain

A 5,596-meter mountain with 13 peaks resembling a jade dragon lying on its side, considered sacred by the Naxi people who believe it protects their souls after death. Your guide arranges a cable car to the Glacier Park at 4,506 meters where you can walk on year-round snow, explains the Naxi custom of lovers who commit suicide together on the mountain to ensure eternal reunion, and points out the rare rhododendrons that bloom at 3,000+ meters.

Black Dragon Pool (Heilongtan)

A park built in 1737 around a spring-fed pond that perfectly reflects Jade Dragon Snow Mountain on clear days — one of the most photographed views in China. Your guide explains the Naxi legend of the black dragon who guarded the spring, the classical pavilions built in the Ming Dynasty style, and the Dongba Cultural Research Institute where you can watch shaman priests perform ancient ceremonies using drums and swords.
