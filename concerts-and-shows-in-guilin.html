桂林千古情 (Guilin Eternal Love)大型演出

Guilin's spectacular large-scale theatrical show — depicting the history and legends of the Guilin region with pyrotechnics, aerial performances, and hundreds of local cast members. Performed nightly.

🎤

桂林漓江之声 (Guilin Li River Folk Song Cruises)

Live performances of traditional Zhuang and Yao folk songs during Li River boat cruises — singers perform from nearby boats as you cruise past the karst peaks. Included in some cruise packages.

🎸

Guilin Music Festival

Held annually (usually October) — classical music, jazz, and world music performances in the karst landscape setting. Both outdoor and indoor venues.

🎻

Guilin Theatre Performances

Regular performances of traditional Chinese opera, dance, and theater at the Guilin Theatre. Traditional Zhuang and Yao minority performances included.

🎭

阳朔西街 (Yangshuo West Street) Evening Buskers

Yangshuo's famous West Street comes alive every evening with informal performances — buskers, traditional instrument players, and street artists. Free to watch.

🎤

Guilin University Cultural Centre Performances

The university hosts regular cultural performances, traditional music recitals, and theater productions open to visitors. Usually free or low cost.
