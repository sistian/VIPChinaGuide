🌙 Nightlife in Chengdu

hengdu never sleeps — Sichuan's spicy cuisine and tea culture make for a uniquely vibrant nightlife scene. Here's where locals eat, drink, and enjoy themselves after dark.

🔥 Top Nightlife & Late-Night Food Picks

🍲

Jian Lane (建路) Night Food Street

hengdu's most famous 24-hour food street — spicy hot pot, cold noodles,串串 (Sichuan BBQ skewers), and cold beer. Locals start eating here after 9pm.

🍜

Kuanzhai Alley (宽窄巷子) Evening

The beautifully restored Qing dynasty pedestrian street is enchanting at night with red lanterns, traditional teahouses, and local snack stalls.

🍺

339 Television Tower Bars

South of the river, the 339 area has Chengdu's liveliest cluster of rooftop bars, breweries, and clubs with views over the city skyline.

🍵

Tianfu Square Night Stroll

The city center square surrounded by neon lights — walk past the iconic giant panda sculpture and the city's grand government buildings lit up at night.

🔥

Sichuan Hot Pot After Midnight

Chengdu's signature dining experience is even better at night — join locals at a late-night hot pot restaurant near Chunxi Road. Ma-La numbingly good at 1am.

🎭

Shu Feng Yue Yun Sichuan Opera

Evening traditional Sichuan opera shows with face-changing Bian Lian performances — one of Chengdu's most unforgettable cultural experiences.
