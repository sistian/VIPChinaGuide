🎭 Concerts and Shows in Shenzhen

Shenzhen's status as China's newest megacity brings cutting-edge performances — from EDM festivals to Broadway touring productions and some of the country's most innovative music venues.

🎵 Top Performances in Shenzhen

🎸

Shenzhen Music Festival (深圳音乐节)

Held annually (usually October) — multi-day outdoor music festival with multiple stages. Electronic, pop, rock, and indie acts from China and Asia. One of southern China's most anticipated festivals.

🎭

Shenzhen Concert Hall Performances

The city's premier classical music venue — Shenzhen Symphony Orchestra concerts, international soloists, and chamber music. Excellent acoustics.

🏛️

Shenzhen Culture Centre Opera House

Large-scale opera, ballet, and Broadway touring productions in the futuristic Culture Centre building. International touring shows frequently appear here.

🎤

B10 Livehouse Shenzhen Concerts

One of China's most respected independent music venues — indie rock, electronic, and experimental acts. International touring bands often stop here. Cover from CNY 100-250.

🎪

Shenzhen International Comedy Festival

Held annually (usually December) — stand-up comedy in English and Chinese, sketch shows, and improv performances. Growing rapidly in popularity.

🎻

Shenzhen Bay Sports Centre Arena Concerts

Major arena shows — international pop and rock performers, Chinese A-list celebrities, and sporting events. The largest indoor venue in the Pearl River Delta.
