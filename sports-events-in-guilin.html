Top Sports Events in Guilin

🏃

Guilin International Marathon (桂林马拉松)

ne of China's most scenic marathons in November. Course winds through karst mountain scenery along the Li River. Full marathon, half marathon, and fun run. The limestone peak backdrop makes this one of the world's most photographed marathons.

Full name: Guilin International Marathon (桂林马拉松)

🚴

Tour of Guangxi - Guilin Stage (环广西自行车赛)

UCI WorldTour race through Guangxi including Guilin. Multi-stage race through karst landscape. Usually October. Top WorldTeams and national teams compete. One of only two UCI WorldTour events in China.

Full name: Tour of Guangxi (环广西公路自行车世界巡回赛)

🏃

Guilin-Yangshuo Mountain Bike Race

Mountain bike race through dramatic karst terrain between Guilin and Yangshuo. Technical single-track through rice paddies and limestone peaks. Popular with Asian mountain biking community. Usually April.

Full name: Guilin-Yangshuo Mountain Bike Challenge

🏊

Li River Open Water Swimming

Organized open water swimming in the Li River near Guilin city. Swimmers pass iconic karst mountain scenery while competing. Usually held in warmer months with beautiful natural backdrop.

Full name: Li River Open Water Swimming Event (漓江游泳赛)

🏃

Guilin Marathon - Li River Stage

Multi-day running event combining marathon with cultural site visits. Participants run through Longji Rice Terraces and rural Guangxi villages. Combines endurance with cultural immersion.

Full name: Guilin Li River Stage Race

🥾

Guilin Karst Trail Running Festival

Trail running event through Guilin karst landscape. Multiple distances from 10km to 100km ultra. Some of China's most spectacular trail running scenery with karst peaks as backdrop. Usually October.

Full name: Guilin Karst Trail Running Festival

Past Results and Future Outlook

Looking back at recent years and what to expect in 2026-2027

2024-2026

Recent Highlights

Guilin Marathon 2026 attracted 25,000 runners including record international participation. The Tour of Guangxi 2026 featured all 18 UCI WorldTeams for the first time since 2019. The karst trail running festival had 3,000 participants across all categories.

2026

2026 Season Outlook

Guilin Marathon 2026 continues to grow as one of China's most scenic running events. The Tour of Guangxi 2026 will feature the full UCI WorldTour calendar with top international teams. New mountain bike routes are being developed in the Yangshuo area.

2027

2027 and Beyond

Guilin is establishing itself as China's adventure sports capital. New ultra-trail events are planned in themao Mountain area. The Li River swimming event is seeking international open water federation recognition as a formal competition.
