Top Sports Events in Shenzhen

🏃

Shenzhen International Marathon (深圳国际马拉松)

nnual marathon in December. The flat coastal course passes Shenzhen Bay, the Shenzhen Stock Exchange, and Qianhai area. IAAF certified course known for excellent organization and fast times. Growing international participation.

Full name: Shenzhen International Marathon (深圳国际马拉松)

🏀

Shenzhen Ningrion Panthers - CBA Basketball

CBA team at Shenzhen Bay Sports Centre. Regular season October to April. The club has invested in international players each season.

Full name: Shenzhen Ningrion Panthers (深圳马可波罗)

⚽

Shenzhen FC - Chinese Super League

Chinese Super League team at Shenzhen Universiade Sports Centre. Season March-November. The club has attracted international talent in recent seasons.

Full name: Shenzhen FC (深圳新鹏城)

🏃

Shenzhen Bay Running Festival

Annual running festival in Shenzhen Bay area. Half marathon, 10km, and 5km categories. Scenic coastal route with views of Hong Kong mountains. Attracts thousands of runners from across the Pearl River Delta.

Full name: Shenzhen Bay Running Festival (深圳湾跑步节)

🎾

Shenzhen Open (深圳网球公开赛)

WTA Challenger tournament at Shenzhen Longgang Sports Centre. Usually September. Emerging women players and established pros compete. Prize money attracting stronger international fields.

Full name: Shenzhen Open (深圳网球公开赛)

🏊

Shenzhen Open Water Swimming Festival

Open water swimming events in Dapeng Peninsula and Shenzhen Bay waters. Multiple distances from 1km to 5km. Usually held in warmer months with spectacular coastal scenery.

Full name: Shenzhen Open Water Swimming Festival (深圳公开水域游泳节)
