🎭 Concerts and Shows in Chengdu

hengdu's performing arts scene is one of the most vibrant in western China — from the famous face-changing Bian Lian shows to outdoor music festivals under the Sichuan sun.

🎵 Top Performances in Chengdu

🎭

Shu Feng Yue Yun — Sichuan Opera (蜀风雅韵)

hengdu's most famous traditional performance — face-changing Bian Lian (变脸), fire-spitting, sleeve dances, and puppet shows. Nightly performances in a beautiful traditional theater setting. An absolute must-see.

🎸

Chengdu Open Air Music Festival (成都露天音乐公园)

Held at the Chengdu Open Air Music Park — major Chinese indie and rock festivals, electronic music events, and large-scale concerts. Usually Spring and Autumn.

🎤

Chengdu People's Art Theatre Performances

The city's main theater for drama, contemporary dance, and experimental theater productions. International touring shows occasionally perform here.

🏟️

Chengdu Phoenix International Mountain Sports Centre Concerts

Major arena concerts — Chinese pop and rock artists, and occasional international performers. Check local ticketing for upcoming shows.

🎻

Chengdu Symphony Orchestra Performances

Regular performances at the Chengdu Grand Theatre — classical repertoire, film scores, and Chinese compositions. Occasional international guest soloists.

🍵

Chengdu Traditional Tea House Cultural Shows

Evening cultural shows in traditional tea houses — traditional instrumental music, Sichuan opera excerpts, and tea ceremony demonstrations in a relaxed setting.
