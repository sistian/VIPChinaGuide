Sports Events in Qingdao

Major sporting events in Qingdao - from world-class marathons to international tournaments. Complete guide with past results and future outlook.

Top Sports Events in Qingdao

⛵

Qingdao International Sailing Week (青岛国际帆船周)

Annual sailing regatta each August at Qingdao. Olympic-class competitions plus recreational events. Qingdao's coastal waters with consistent winds are ideal for sailing. Part of the city's 2008 and 2022 Olympic legacy.

Full name: Qingdao International Sailing Week (青岛国际帆船周)

🏃

Qingdao International Marathon (青岛国际马拉松)

Annual marathon in November. The coastal course passes Zhanqiao Pier, Badaguan European architecture district, and Yellow Sea coastline. One of China's most scenic seaside marathons with European-influenced city backdrop.

Full name: Qingdao International Marathon (青岛国际马拉松)

🏊

Qingdao Beach Swimming Festival

Open water swimming festival at Qingdao's famous bathing beaches. Multiple distance events in the Yellow Sea. Popular summer event for local and visiting swimmers. Multiple beaches host events over the summer season.

Full name: Qingdao Beach Swimming Festival (青岛海滨游泳节)

⚽

Qingdao Huanghai FC - Chinese Super League

Chinese Super League football. Season March-November. Coastal city football with passionate fan culture. The club has played in both CSL and China League One.

Full name: Qingdao Huanghai / Qingdao West Coast FC

🏃

Laoshan Mountain Marathon

Marathon and trail race in the Laoshan Mountain area - China's coastal highest point and birthplace of Taoism. Challenging mountain terrain with dramatic coastal views. Combines running with Taoist cultural experience.

Full name: Laoshan Mountain Marathon (崂山马拉松)

🚴

Qingdao Coastal Cycling Marathon

Long-distance cycling event along Qingdao's coastline. Route passes major coastal landmarks and scenic roads. Open to international cycling enthusiasts. Multiple distance categories.

Full name: Qingdao Coastal Cycling Marathon (青岛海岸骑行赛)

Past Results and Future Outlook

Looking back at recent years and what to expect in 2026-2027

2024-2025

Recent Highlights

Qingdao International Sailing Week 2025 was the largest edition yet with 200+ boats participating. Qingdao Marathon 2025 attracted 30,000 runners. Qingdao Huanghai FC achieved promotion back to the Chinese Super League for the 2025 season.

2026

2026 Season Outlook

Qingdao Sailing Week 2026 is expanding its international fleet participation. The marathon continues to grow as a major coastal running event. Qingdao FC aims to establish itself in the CSL mid-table.

2027

2027 and Beyond

Qingdao is developing Laoshan as a year-round mountain sports destination. New sailing events are being added including a winter sailing series. The city is preparing a bid for a World Championship sailing event.
