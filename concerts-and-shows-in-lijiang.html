🎭 Concerts and Shows in Lijiang

ijiang's evenings are filled with traditional Naxi ancient music, minority cultural performances, and live shows set against the backdrop of the UNESCO old town and Jade Dragon Snow Mountain.

🎵 Top Performances in Lijiang

🎶

Lijiang Naxi Ancient Music Concert (纳西古乐)

he world-famous Lijiang Naxi Ancient Music Orchestra performs centuries-old melodies that predate the Ming dynasty. Performed nightly in the old town — UNESCO-listed and considered one of China's most precious musical traditions. Book through your guide.

🏔️

印象丽江 (Impression Lijiang) — Open-Air Show

Staged at the foot of Jade Dragon Snow Mountain, this spectacular open-air performance involves hundreds of local Naxi and Yi performers. One of China's most impressive cultural shows. Usually performed March–November weather permitting.

🎭

Lijiang Tinghua Night Performance

Traditional Naxi and Yunnan minority cultural evening performances in the old town's main theater — song, dance, and traditional instrument showcases. Usually start at 8pm nightly.

🎸

Lijiang Old Town Live Music Bars

The old town's bar street has live music venues every night — local bands, indie musicians, and traveling artists. Intimate settings with CNY 20-50 cover charge.

🥁

Naxi Folk Music Heritage Centre Shows

Daily performances of traditional Naxi music by heritage musicians — including the ancient Dongba ritual music passed down through generations. Afternoon and evening shows available.

🎤

Lijiang International Song and Dance Festival

Held annually (usually September), this festival brings together minority song and dance troupes from across Yunnan and Southwest China. Multi-day event with performances across the old town.
