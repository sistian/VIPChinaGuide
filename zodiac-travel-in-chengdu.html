Frequently Asked Questions About Chinese Zodiac Travel

What is the Chinese zodiac (十二生肖)?
The Chinese zodiac is a 12-year cycle where each year is associated with one of 12 animals: Rat (鼠), Ox (牛), Tiger (虎), Rabbit (兔), Dragon (龙), Snake (蛇), Horse (马), Goat (羊), Monkey (猴), Rooster (鸡), Dog (狗), Pig (猪). Your zodiac sign is determined by your birth year in the Chinese lunar calendar.
Which zodiac sign is best for adventure travel to Chengdu?
Tiger (虎) and Horse (马) are the most adventure-oriented signs. For Chengdu, Tiger travelers will love mountain hiking and extreme activities, while Horse travelers thrive with spontaneous outdoor roaming and social experiences.
Which zodiac signs prefer slow travel in Chengdu?
Rabbit (兔) and Goat (羊) are born for slow, serene travel. In Chengdu, they should seek hot spring resorts, lakeside retreats, and peaceful cultural sites away from crowds.
