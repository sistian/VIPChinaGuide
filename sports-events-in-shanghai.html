⚽ Shanghai Sports Events Guide

Major sporting events in Shanghai - from world-class marathons to international tournaments. Complete guide with past results and future outlook.

Top Sports Events in Shanghai

🎾

Shanghai Rolex Masters (上海劳力士大师赛)

ATP Masters 1000 tennis at Qizhong Stadium - one of nine ATP Masters events globally. Usually October. World No. 1 players compete. Prize money over USD 1 million. The stadium's roof allows play in all weather.

Full name: Shanghai Rolex Masters (上海劳力士大师赛)

🏎️

F1 Chinese Grand Prix (中国大奖赛)

Formula 1 World Championship race at Shanghai International Circuit (上海国际赛车场). Usually April. The 5.4km circuit designed by Hermann Tilke features the iconic 'Dragon Tail' sequence of corners. First held 2004, returned to calendar 2024 after pandemic pause.

Full name: FIA Formula 1 Chinese Grand Prix (中国大奖赛)

🏃

Shanghai International Marathon (上海国际马拉松)

IAAF Gold Label certified marathon usually held in November. Full course passes the Bund, Huangpu River crossing, French Concession, and Xintiandi. One of China's flattest and fastest marathon courses. International runners from 80+ countries participate.

Full name: Shanghai International Marathon (上海国际马拉松)

⚽

Shanghai Shenhua & SIPG - Chinese Super League

Two Chinese Super League teams - Shenhua at Hongkou Stadium and SIPG at Shanghai Oriental Sports Center. Season March-November. International players join each transfer window. SIPG has won the league title multiple times.

Full name: Shanghai Shenhua / Shanghai Port FC

🏀

Shanghai Sharks - CBA Basketball

Yao Ming's former CBA team at Shanghai Oriental Sports Center. Regular season October to April. Known for developing young Chinese talent.

Full name: Shanghai Sharks (上海大鲨鱼)

🏊

Shanghai Open Water Swimming Championship

Annual open water event in the Huangpu River. Athletes swim past the Bund with iconic city views. Usually October. Multiple distance categories.

Full name: Shanghai Open Water Swimming Championship (上海公开水域游泳锦标赛)

Past Results and Future Outlook

Looking back at recent years and what to expect in 2026-2027

2024-2025

Recent Highlights

F1 Chinese Grand Prix returned in 2024 after pandemic with record attendance - over 200,000 spectators across three days. Shanghai Marathon 2025 celebrated its 30th anniversary with record 38,000 runners. The Shanghai Rolex Masters 2025 drew all top-10 ATP players.

2026

2026 Season Outlook

F1 Chinese Grand Prix 2026 confirmed for April at Shanghai International Circuit. Shanghai Marathon 2026 expects to return to full international participation. Shanghai SIPG continues to strengthen its squad for both CSL and AFC Champions League competitions.

2027

2027 and Beyond

Shanghai continues bidding for additional major international events. The Shanghai Marathon is seeking World Athletics Platinum Label status. New sports venues are under development in the Lingang area as part of the city's sports infrastructure expansion plans.
