🌙 Nightlife in Dali

ali nightlife is legendary among backpackers — lakeside bars, live music and a festive atmosphere until early hours.

🔥 Top Nightlife Picks in Dali

🍺

Dali Old Town Bars and Live Music

arrow streets packed with bars — live music venues, cocktail bars and casual backpacker pubs. Best atmosphere in Yunnan.

🌙

Erhai Lake Moonrise

Most romantic experience in Dali — watching the moon rise over Erhai Lake from the eastern shore, best from Shuanglang.

🍜

Dali Old Town Night Market Food

Night market every evening — Bai minority snacks, grilled cheese, local noodles and cheap beer at outdoor tables.

🎵

Catherine Bar and Dali Live Music

30-year Dali institution — live music, bonfires and legendary atmosphere drawing travelers worldwide.

🌊

Dali Lakeside Deng Bars

Boutique bars near Erhai Lake — quieter than old town with lake views and relaxed vibe.

🍵

Dali Traditional Tea House Night

Traditional tea houses in old town where you can sample Dali tie-guan-yin tea in a peaceful setting.
