🌙 Nightlife in Hangzhou

angzhou nights are magical — West Lake under stars, bustling food streets, and a thriving bar scene in the former concession area.

🔥 Top Nightlife Picks in Hangzhou

🌸

West Lake at Night

ne of China most romantic nighttime walks — illuminated causeways and pagodas. Boat tours until 9pm.

🍜

Hefang Street Evening (河坊街)

Beautifully restored ancient street at its most atmospheric in evening — red lanterns, traditional snacks. Open until 10pm.

🍺

Shuguang Road Bar District

Most popular bar area — boutique cocktail bars, craft beer pubs and wine lounges in renovated traditional buildings.

🍵

Longjing Tea House Night

For a calmer evening — traditional tea houses near West Lake where you can drink Longjing tea under stars.

🌿

Xiang Lake Night Market

Newer lakeside food stalls with international and local cuisines, popular with young Hangzhou creative crowd.

🎵

West Lake Cultural Square Evening

Free outdoor cultural performances near the lake — traditional music and dancing in open-air setting.
