🎭 Concerts and Shows in Urumqi

rumqi's performance culture reflects its position at the crossroads of the Silk Road — Uyghur classical music, Central Asian dance, and the famous Twelve Muqam are among the most extraordinary performances in China.

🎵 Top Performances in Urumqi

🎵

新疆木卡姆 (Xinjiang Twelve Muqam Performances)

he Uyghur Twelve Muqam is a UNESCO-listed art form — a complex fusion of music, poetry, song, and dance. Regular performances at the Xinjiang Theatre and cultural centers. A profound cultural experience.

💃

新疆民族歌舞 (Xinjiang Ethnic Song and Dance Performances)

Spectacular evening shows featuring traditional Uyghur, Kazakh, and Hui dance and music. Performed at major theaters and cultural venues — elaborate costumes, energetic dancing, and haunting melodies.

🎸

丝路之心 (Silk Road Heart)大型演出

The signature large-scale cultural performance in Urumqi — a theatrical show depicting the Silk Road history of Xinjiang with live music, dance, and visual effects. Usually performed at the Xinjiang Grand Theatre.

🎻

Urumqi International Music Festival

Held annually (usually July/August), featuring classical music performances by international and Chinese orchestras in the city's concert halls. Summer timing coincides with Xinjiang's best travel weather.

🏟️

Urumqi Sports Centre Concerts

Major pop and rock concerts are held at the Urumqi Sports Centre — international and Chinese pop artists occasionally perform in the city. Check local listings closer to your visit.

🎤

南疆大巴扎 (Southern Xinjiang Grand Bazaar) Cultural Shows

Evening cultural performances in the Grand Bazaar complex — traditional music, dance, and Uyghur cultural showcases. Open to all visitors, usually included in the bazaar admission.
