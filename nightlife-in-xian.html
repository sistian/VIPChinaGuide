🌙 Nightlife in Xi'an

i'an's nightlife is anchored by its iconic ancient city wall, a legendary food street, and a Muslim Quarter that comes alive after dark. Here's where to experience it.

🔥 Top Nightlife & Late-Night Food Picks

🕌

Muslim Quarter Evening Walk

he most atmospheric evening in western China — the 1,000-year-old mosque surrounded by baba ovens, roujamo stalls, and silk road tea houses. Open until 11pm.

🍜

East Street (东大街) Night Food

The historic commercial street is beautifully lit at night with hundreds of restaurants, noodle shops, and snack vendors serving late into the night.

🏯

City Wall Night Views

The ancient Ming dynasty city wall is dramatically lit at night — rent a bike or take an evening walk along the 14-km perimeter for panoramic night views of the city.

🍺

Bell and Drum Tower Square

The area between the Bell and Drum Towers is vibrant in the evening — food stalls, musicians, and local performers gather under the illuminated towers.

🥙

Late-Night Biang Biang Noodles

Xi'an's signature wide noodles are available 24 hours — try the old noodle kitchens near South Street that have been serving night owls for decades.

🍻

Daxingxi Market Nightlife

The newly developed Daxingxi area has Xi'an's newest bars, breweries, and cocktail lounges — popular with the young creative crowd.
