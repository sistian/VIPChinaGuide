🚆 Zhangjiajie Transport Options

🚌

Zhangjiajie City Transport

No metro. City transport by bus and taxi. Tourist shuttles between attractions.

Zhangjiajie Taxi Fares

Starts CNY 6 for 2 km, CNY 1.5/km. For Wulingyuan (30 km), take tourist bus or hire car.

DiDi Ride-Hailing (滴滴出行)

Available in city. For attractions outside center, taxi or tour is best.

Zhangjiajie Train Stations

Zhangjiajie Station (张家界站) — classic trains. Zhangjiajie West (张家界西) — HSR: to Changsha (3h), Guiyang, and major cities.

Zhangjiajie Long-Distance Bus

Zhangjiajie Bus Station (张家界汽车站): buses to Fenghuang (3h), Changsha (4h), Wulingyuan (1h).
