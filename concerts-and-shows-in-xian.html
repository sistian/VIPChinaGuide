🎭 Concerts and Shows in Xi'an

i'an's performance culture draws from 3,000 years of history — from Tang Dynasty music and dance to modern indie rock. Here's where to find the best shows in this ancient capital.

🎵 Top Performances in Xi'an

🎭

长恨歌 (Eternal Sorrow) Outdoor Performance

hina's most spectacular outdoor historical performance — staged at the Huaqing Pool (华清池) where Emperor Xuanzong and Yang Guifei's love story unfolded. Full-scale pyrotechnics, fountains, and hundreds of performers. Usually April–October.

🎸

Xi'an International Music Festival

Held annually (usually September/October) — classical music, jazz, and world music performances in the city's historic venues including the Giant Wild Goose Pagoda grounds.

🎤

Sandal Box Live House and Indie Music Venues

Xi'an's main indie music venue — local Chinese indie bands, rock acts, and touring underground artists. Small cover charge. The indie scene has grown rapidly in recent years.

🏛️

Xi'an Song and Dance Theatre Performances

Traditional Tang Dynasty music and dance (唐代乐舞) — elegant recreations of the court performances from the Tang Dynasty era. Nightly performances in restored Tang-style theaters.

🎭

Qujiang Concert Hall Performances

Modern concert hall in the Qujiang district — classical music, opera, and international touring productions. Check the monthly schedule.

🎤

Xi'an MAO Livehouse Concerts

One of China's best independent music venues — touring indie and rock bands from across China and beyond. Small to mid-size shows.
