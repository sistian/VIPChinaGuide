Top Sports Events in Dali

🚴

Tour of Dali - UCI Asia Tour (大理tour)

nternational UCI Asia Tour cycling race through Dali and Erhai Lake area. Multiple stages through Bai ethnic region. Usually March. Top Asian and international cycling teams compete. One of China's premier stage races.

Full name: Tour of Dali (环大理自行车赛)

🏃

Dali Erhai Lake Marathon (大理洱海马拉松)

Marathon and half marathon around Erhai Lake (洱海). Scenic course with Erhai Lake on one side and Cangshan Mountains on the other. International participants combine race with Dali's cultural attractions. Usually November.

Full name: Dali Erhai Lake Marathon (大理洱海马拉松)

🏔️

Dali High Altitude Mountain Bike Race

Mountain bike race at altitude in the Dali area. Technical terrain through Yunnan highland scenery. Attracts regional mountain biking enthusiasts. Usually April.

Full name: Dali High Altitude MTB Race (大理高原山地自行车赛)

🏃

Dali Ancient Town Half Marathon

Half marathon through Dali's famous ancient town. Course passes Bai minority architecture and the Three Pagodas. Popular with tourists combining running with cultural visits. Usually March.

Full name: Dali Ancient Town Half Marathon (大理古城半程马拉松)

🚣

Erhai Lake Rowing Race

Traditional boat rowing race on Erhai Lake. Local teams compete in traditional long boats. Cultural event celebrating Yunnan's water heritage. Usually April.

Full name: Erhai Lake Traditional Rowing Race (洱海划船赛)

🏃

Cangshan Mountain Hiking Race

Trail hiking and running event up Cangshan Mountain. Steep climbs rewarded with panoramic views of Erhai Lake and Dali valley. Multiple difficulty levels. Usually October.

Full name: Cangshan Mountain Hiking Race (苍山徒步赛)

Past Results and Future Outlook

Looking back at recent years and what to expect in 2026-2027

2024-2026

Recent Highlights

Tour of Dali 2026 was the largest edition with 20 teams from 15 countries. The Dali Marathon attracted 15,000 runners in 2026 including record international participants. The Erhai Lake rowing race was revived after a pandemic pause.

2026

2026 Season Outlook

Tour of Dali 2026 is confirmed on the UCI Asia Tour calendar. The Dali Marathon continues to grow in popularity among international runners. New mountain bike routes are being developed in the Dali area.

2027

2027 and Beyond

Dali is establishing itself as Yunnan province's sports events capital. New events including a Dali extreme sports festival are planned for 2027. The Erhai Lake area is being developed as a year-round sports destination.
