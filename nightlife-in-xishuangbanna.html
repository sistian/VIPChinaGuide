Manting Park Night Cultural Show

vening cultural performances — traditional Dai dances, water splashing celebrations and tropical music under the stars.

Jinghong Night Market (景洪夜市)

Most atmospheric night market in Yunnan — Dai and Burmese cuisine, tropical fruits, grilled fish and cold beer by the river.

🍺

Lancang River Riverside Bars

Riverside area near Jinghong has growing cluster of bars and cafes — outdoor seating with river breezes.

🍲

Dai-Style Late-Night Hot Pot

Tropical version of hot pot — lighter broths, fresh tropical vegetables and Dai specialty dipping sauces. Open until 11pm.

🌺

Gongmao Night Flower Market

Tropical flower market comes alive in evening — colorful blooms, tropical fruits and local snacks.

🎵

Jinghong International Bar Scene

Surprising range of bars — from local Dai beer gardens to international cocktail lounges.
