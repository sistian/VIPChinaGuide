Top Sports Events in Wuhan

🏃

Wuhan Marathon (武汉马拉松)

AAF Gold Label certified marathon in April. Course crosses the Yangtze and Han Rivers, passes Yellow Crane Tower, and showcases Wuhan's famous East Lake. Known for its flat, fast course and enthusiastic crowds. One of central China's premier marathons.

Full name: Wuhan Marathon (武汉马拉松)

🏊

Wuhan International Swimming Festival (武汉国际渡江节)

Annual open water crossing of the Yangtze River - a Wuhan tradition since 1956. Thousands of swimmers participate from Wuluo Island to Hanyang. A unique experience swimming in China's longest river. Usually July.

Full name: Wuhan International Crossing of the Yangtze (武汉国际渡江节)

⚽

Wuhan Three Towns FC - Chinese Super League

Wuhan's Chinese Super League team and back-to-back league champions. Home matches at Wuhan Sports Centre. Season March-November. Known for developing young Chinese talent alongside experienced international players.

Full name: Wuhan Three Towns FC (武汉三镇)

🏀

Wuhan Peng Chao - CBA Basketball

CBA basketball team at Wuhan Sports Centre. Regular season October to April. Emerging young talent alongside experienced international players.

Full name: Wuhan Pengchao (武汉锟鹏)

🏃

East Lake Rowing Championship

International rowing competition at East Lake (东湖) - China's largest city lake. Universities and national teams compete in various boat classes. Usually April. Beautiful setting surrounded by mountains.

Full name: East Lake Rowing Championship (东湖赛艇锦标赛)

🏃

Wuhan Night Marathon

Evening marathon event around East Lake. Unique night race with illuminated lake and city views. Growing in popularity among local and regional runners. Usually October.

Full name: Wuhan Night Marathon (武汉夜跑马拉松)

Past Results and Future Outlook

Looking back at recent years and what to expect in 2026-2027

2024-2026

Recent Highlights

Wuhan Marathon 2026 was the largest edition with 30,000 runners. Wuhan Three Towns FC won the Chinese Super League in 2024 - the first time a Wuhan club won the top tier. The Yangtze swimming festival attracted over 10,000 swimmers in 2026.

2026

2026 Season Outlook

Wuhan Marathon 2026 continues to grow with expanded international elite runner invitations. Wuhan Three Towns FC continues building for both domestic and AFC Champions League competitions. The East Lake rowing events are expanding with new university participation.

2027

2027 and Beyond

Wuhan is emerging as central China's sports capital. New sports venues are under construction including an expanded rowing facility at East Lake. The Yangtze crossing festival is applying for World Open Water Swimming Association recognition.
