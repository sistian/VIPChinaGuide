Sports Events in Xishuangbanna

Major sporting events in Xishuangbanna - from world-class marathons to international tournaments. Complete guide with past results and future outlook.

Top Sports Events in Xishuangbanna

🏃

Xishuangbanna Tropical Jungle Marathon (西双版纳热带雨林马拉松)

Marathon and half marathon through tropical rainforest scenery. Hot and humid conditions at low altitude. Unique tropical running experience in China's southernmost region. Usually December-January when weather is coolest.

Full name: Xishuangbanna Tropical Jungle Marathon (西双版纳马拉松)

🏊

Mekong River Open Water Swim

Open water swimming in the Mekong River (澜沧江) near Jinghong. International swimmers compete in the lower Mekong - Asia's seventh longest river. Unique opportunity to swim one of Asia's major rivers. Usually November.

Full name: Mekong River Open Water Swim (澜沧江游泳赛)

🚴

Xishuangbanna Tropical Cycling Race

Multi-stage cycling through tropical scenery. Routes pass Dai minority villages, rubber plantations, and rainforest areas. UCI Asia Tour registered event. Usually March.

Full name: Xishuangbanna Tropical Cycling Race (西双版纳自行车赛)

🏃

Wild Elephant Valley Trail Race

Trail running near Wild Elephant Valley nature reserve. Course through tropical forest with possibility of wild elephant sightings. Challenging tropical terrain and humidity. Usually December.

Full name: Wild Elephant Valley Trail Race (野象谷越野赛)

🏃

Jinghong Riverside Marathon

Marathon along the Mekong River in Jinghong city. Flat scenic course along the river with tropical vegetation. Warmer months are challenging but the tropical scenery rewards effort. Usually April.

Full name: Jinghong Riverside Marathon (景洪滨江马拉松)

🐘

Xishuangbanna Elephant Polo Tournament

Unique elephant polo event in the Xishuangbanna area - one of few places in China where elephant polo is practiced. Combines traditional elephant culture with sport. Exhibition format - check schedule locally.

Full name: Xishuangbanna Elephant Polo Tournament (版纳象球赛)

Past Results and Future Outlook

Looking back at recent years and what to expect in 2026-2027

2024-2025

Recent Highlights

Xishuangbanna Marathon 2025 attracted 20,000 runners including record international participation. The Mekong open water swim had swimmers from 18 countries. The tropical cycling race expanded to four stages in 2025.

2026

2026 Season Outlook

Xishuangbanna Marathon 2026 continues to grow as a major tropical destination marathon. New trail running events are being developed in the Menglian area. The elephant polo exhibition is expanding with more performances scheduled.

2027

2027 and Beyond

Xishuangbanna is establishing itself as China's tropical sports destination. New international marathon events are planned for the tropical lowland areas. The Mekong swimming event is seeking international open water federation recognition.
