重庆川剧 (Chongqing Sichuan Opera) Performances

Sichuan Opera — face-changing Bian Lian (变脸), fire-spitting, and acrobatics — performed at the Chongqing Sichuan Opera Theatre. Nightly performances in an intimate theater setting.

🎸

重庆音乐节 (Chongqing Music Festival)

Held annually (usually October) — multi-genre outdoor music festival with Chinese and Asian indie, rock, and electronic acts. Multi-day event at outdoor venues near the Jialing River.

🎤

Chongqing Grand Theatre Performances

The city's main performing arts venue — ballet, opera, Chinese classical music, and touring theatrical productions. Check monthly schedule through your guide.

🏟️

重庆奥体中心 (Chongqing Olympic Sports Centre) Concerts

Major arena concerts — Chinese pop and rock artists, and occasional international performers. One of southwest China's largest venues.

🎭

两江之夜 (Two Rivers Night) Cultural Cruise Shows

Evening performances on the Yangtze and Jialing River — traditional Chinese music, dance, and cultural performances combined with the city's spectacular night views. Usually combined with dinner cruise.

🎸

Chongqing Indie Music Venues

A growing indie and underground music scene — local rock bands, indie acts, and occasional touring performers at small venues in the Jiefangbei and Yuzhou districts.
