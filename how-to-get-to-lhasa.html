Lhasa Railway Station

Lhasa Station (拉萨站): Terminates Qinghai-Tibet Railway — world's highest plateau railway. To Beijing (40h), Xining (24h), Chengdu (36h). Station is 20 km from center — taxi CNY 40-60.

Lhasa Long-Distance Bus

Lhasa North Suburb Bus Station (拉萨北郊客运站): buses to Shigatse (5h), Nyingchi (10h). Tibet Travel Permit (入藏函) required for foreign nationals.

How to Buy Train & Bus Tickets

Train tickets sell out months ahead — book through 12306.cn, Trip.com, or railway bureau. Tibet permit mandatory — obtain through registered Tibet tour operator.
