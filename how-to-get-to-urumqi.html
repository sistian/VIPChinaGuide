Urumqi Train Stations

Urumqi Station (乌鲁木齐站) — main hub: HSR to Beijing (30h), Shanghai (35h), Lanzhou (12h). Urumqi South (乌鲁木齐南) — classic to Kashgar (14h), Turpan (2h), Hotan.

Urumqi Long-Distance Bus

Urumqi Nanjiao Bus Station (乌鲁木齐市南郊客运站): daily buses to Turpan (2h), Karamay (4h), Kashgar (12h).
