Top Sports Events in Lhasa

🏃

Lhasa Marathon (拉萨马拉松)

igh-altitude marathon in the world's highest major city at 3,650m. Course passes the Potala Palace, Jokhang Temple, and Barkhor Street. A bucket-list event for endurance runners worldwide. Usually April.

Full name: Lhasa Marathon (拉萨马拉松)

🏃

Qinghai-Tibet Plateau Marathon

Ultra marathon at extreme altitude. One of world's highest altitude running events. Attracts elite runners seeking the ultimate high-altitude challenge. Usually May.

Full name: Qinghai-Tibet Plateau Marathon (青藏高原马拉松)

⚽

Tibet Football League Matches

Regional football at Lhasa Sports Stadium. China League Two level football with teams from across the Tibet Autonomous Region. Season May to October at altitude.

Full name: Tibet Autonomous Region Football League (西藏足球联赛)

🏔️

Everest Base Camp Running Events

Tours departing Lhasa for running events at Everest Base Camp at 5,200m altitude. The ultimate high-altitude running experience near the world's highest peak. Usually May-June when weather is most stable.

Full name: Everest Base Camp Running Events (珠峰大本营跑步活动)

🏃

Barkhor Street Walking Challenge

Non-competitive walking event around the sacred Barkhor Street kora circuit. Participants join local pilgrims in this 2km circuit around Jokhang Temple. Cultural event celebrating Tibetan Buddhist tradition. Open to all.

Full name: Barkhor Street Walking Challenge (八廓街步行挑战)

⛷️

Qamdo Skiing and Winter Sports

Ski resorts in the Qamdo area accessible from Lhasa offer skiing and winter sports at altitude. New ski resort development in eastern Tibet. Season December to March.

Full name: Qamdo Ski Resort Activities (昌都滑雪)

Past Results and Future Outlook

Looking back at recent years and what to expect in 2026-2027

2024-2026

Recent Highlights

Lhasa Marathon 2026 was the largest edition with 5,000 runners including international participants. The Everest Base Camp running events resumed in 2026 with increased capacity. Tibet FC had a competitive season in the regional league.

2026

2026 Season Outlook

Lhasa Marathon 2026 is expanding participation capacity with improved international runner support. New Everest Base Camp running events are being planned with more dates available. Tibet is developing new high-altitude training facilities.

2027

2027 and Beyond

Lhasa is becoming a globally recognized high-altitude sports destination. New international high-altitude training camps are being developed. The Barkhor Street walking challenge is being formalized as an annual cultural sports event with international participation.
