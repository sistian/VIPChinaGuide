Guangzhou Metro — 18 Lines

et a 岭南通 card (CNY 50 deposit) for metro + bus across Guangdong. Official app: 广州地铁. WeChat/Alipay QR. Key: Line 2 (to Guangzhou South), Line 5 (Canton Tower), Line 8 (Chen Clan).

Guangzhou Taxi Fares

Starts CNY 12 for 2.5 km, CNY 2.7/km. All metered. During Canton Fair (Apr & Oct) prices surge and availability drops.
