印象西湖 (Impression West Lake) Night Performance

The signature open-air performance on West Lake — a spectacular multimedia show depicting Hangzhou's history and legends, staged directly on the water with the Causeway as backdrop. Usually April–November.

🎸

杭州西湖音乐节 (Hangzhou West Lake Music Festival)

Held annually (usually October) — multi-day music festival with indie rock, folk, and electronic acts on outdoor stages near West Lake.

🎻

杭州大剧院 (Hangzhou Grand Theatre) Performances

Hangzhou's premier performing arts venue — ballet, opera, Hangzhou Philharmonic concerts, and international touring productions.

🎤

Hangzhou Livehouse Indie Music Venues

The indie music scene in Hangzhou's historic neighborhoods — live house venues host Chinese indie bands and touring acts. Small cover charges.

🏛️

浙江音乐学院 (Zhejiang Conservatory) Free Concerts

The conservatory hosts free student concerts, classical performances, and open rehearsals. A wonderful way to experience Chinese classical music in an intimate setting.

🎭

Hangzhou Song Dynasty Cultural Performances

Traditional Song Dynasty music and dance performances in restored Song-style theaters — elegant period costume shows depicting the culture of Hangzhou's most celebrated era.
