🌙 Nightlife in Lijiang

ijiang old town transforms at night — red lanterns light the ancient waterways and Naxi culture blends with vibrant bar and cafe scene.

🔥 Top Nightlife Picks in Lijiang

🏮

Lijiang Old Town at Night

NESCO-listed old town magical at night — lantern-lit canals, traditional Naxi architecture and crowds strolling cobblestone streets.

🍺

Lijiang Old Town Bar Street

Main bar street in old town — live music venues, cocktail bars and local Naxi bands performing until 1am.

🍜

Lijiang Night Market Food

Night market near South Gate every evening — local Naxi and Yunnan cuisine, grilled skewers and fresh fruit.

🎵

Naxi Music Night Performance

Traditional Naxi ancient music performed nightly — haunting UNESCO-listed melodies predating the Ming dynasty.

🌙

Elephant Mountain Sunset and Night View

Hike up Elephant Mountain at sunset for panoramic views over Lijiang and Jade Dragon Snow Mountain.

🍵

Lijiang Traditional Tea House

Quieter alternative — traditional tea houses near old town streams where you can drink Pu-er tea.
