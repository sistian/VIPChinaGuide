武汉草莓音乐节 (Wuhan Strawberry Music Festival)

The Strawberry Music Festival — Wuhan is one of its founding cities. Annual multi-day indie/rock/electronic festival in Spring. One of China's most prestigious indie music events.

🎭

武汉京剧院 (Wuhan Peking Opera) Performances

Traditional Peking Opera performances at the Wuhan Peking Opera Theatre — elaborate costumes, face painting, and the distinctive singing style. Occasional English-subtitled performances.

🎤

Vox Livehouse Wuhan — Legendary Indie Venue

Wuhan's famous indie music venue — Vox has launched some of China's biggest indie bands. Regular live shows by local and touring indie artists. Cover from CNY 80-150.

🎻

武汉交响乐团 (Wuhan Symphony Orchestra) Performances

Regular performances at the Qintai Concert Hall — classical Western repertoire, Chinese compositions, and occasional international soloists. Monthly program.

🏟️

武汉体育中心 (Wuhan Sports Centre) Concerts

Major arena concerts — Chinese pop stars and occasional international performers. The largest indoor venue in central China.

🎤

武汉剧院 (Wuhan People's Theatre) Drama

Drama, contemporary dance, and experimental theater productions at the city's main theater. Also hosts visiting international touring productions.
