Sports Events in Hangzhou

Major sporting events in Hangzhou - from world-class marathons to international tournaments. Complete guide with past results and future outlook.

Top Sports Events in Hangzhou

🏃

Hangzhou Marathon (杭州马拉松)

IAAF Gold Label certified marathon in November. One of China's oldest marathons - first held in 1987. Course passes West Lake, Qiantang River, and modern Hangzhou CBD. Known for beautiful scenery and excellent organization.

Full name: Hangzhou Marathon (杭州马拉松)

🏄

Qiantang River Tidal Bore Surfing (钱塘江潮)

World's largest tidal wave - the Qiantang River bore reaches up to 9 meters. International surfers gather to ride the famous wave in September-October. Standing wave at Haining section is surfable for over an hour. Unforgettable spectator experience from river banks.

Full name: Qiantang River Tidal Bore (钱塘江回头潮)

🏀

Hangzhou Lions - CBA Basketball

CBA basketball team at Hangzhou Diankehui Stadium. Regular season October to April. Strong rivalry with provincial neighbors. The team has a history of championship contention.

Full name: Zhejiang Guangsha / Zhejiang Lions

⚽

Hangzhou 2022 Asian Games Legacy Events

Following the highly successful 2022 Asian Games, Hangzhou continues hosting major events at world-class venues. Swimming, eSports, and team sports venues built for the games remain in active use for national and international competitions.

Full name: Hangzhou 2022 Asian Games (杭州2022年第19届亚运会) Legacy Events

🏃

West Lake Cycling Tour (西湖自行车赛)

Annual cycling event around UNESCO World Heritage West Lake. Multiple distance categories. Flat scenic route around the famous lake. Open to international participants.

Full name: Hangzhou West Lake Cycling Tour

🎯

Hangzhou eSports Masters

Major eSports tournaments at Hangzhou's dedicated eSports venues. China leads global eSports - Hangzhou hosts major Dota 2, League of Legends, and Honor of Kings events. Huge prize pools attract top global teams.

Full name: Hangzhou eSports Masters (杭州电竞大师赛)

Past Results and Future Outlook

Looking back at recent years and what to expect in 2026-2027

2024-2025

Recent Highlights

Hangzhou Marathon 2025 was the largest since its founding with 35,000 runners. The 2022 Asian Games legacy venues continue hosting world-class events - the Hangzhou eSports Masters 2025 had a prize pool of USD 5 million. The Qiantang tidal bore surfing 2025 saw record international participation.

2026

2026 Season Outlook

Hangzhou Marathon 2026 continues its growth trajectory with increased international elite participation. The Asian Games venues are hosting their first full calendar of events in 2026. Hangzhou eSports continues as a global eSports hub with major tournaments planned.

2027

2027 and Beyond

Hangzhou is becoming China's premier sports events city. New international events are being planned using the world-class Asian Games infrastructure. The West Lake cycling event is seeking UCI recognition for its route.
