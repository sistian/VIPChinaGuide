Top Sports Events in Changsha

🏃

Changsha International Marathon (长沙国际马拉松)

nnual marathon in October-November. Course crosses the Xiang River (湘江), passes Orange Island (橘子洲), and the historic streets of Changsha. Known for enthusiastic crowds and the iconic Orange Island headland mid-race.

Full name: Changsha International Marathon (长沙国际马拉松)

⚽

Changsha Yicheng FC - China League One

China League One football at Changsha Helong Stadium. The passionate fan culture makes home games memorable. Season April to October.

Full name: Changsha Yicheng (长沙勇胜)

🏃

Orange Island Marathon (橘子洲马拉松)

Marathon and half marathon held on Orange Island in the Xiang River. Scenic river views with the backdrop of Changsha's modern skyline. The island's pleasant environment makes for a unique urban running experience.

Full name: Orange Island Marathon (橘子洲马拉松)

🏊

Xiang River Open Water Swimming

Annual open water swimming event in the Xiang River. Swimmers cross sections of the river with views of Changsha's modern skyline and historic sites. A popular summer tradition. Usually August.

Full name: Xiang River Open Water Swimming (湘江渡江活动)

🏀

Hunan University Basketball

University basketball at Changsha's top universities. CUBA league matches draw large student crowds. Season runs October to May. The university teams are competitive at the national level.

Full name: CUBA Basketball - Hunan University Teams

🏃

Changsha Marshes Trail Race

Trail running event through the unique marshland terrain near Changsha. Attracts regional runners for its distinctive natural landscape. Multiple distance options.

Full name: Changsha Marshes Trail Race (长沙湿地越野赛)
