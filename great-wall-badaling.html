Why Visit Badaling?

Badaling is the most famous section of the Great Wall — the first section to be opened to tourists, the most visited, and the most crowded. If your time in China is limited and you need to see the Wall with your own eyes, Ba daling is the most accessible option, reachable by bus from Beijing in under two hours. But "seeing" the Great Wall and "experiencing" it are two very different things.

See the Wall. Or experience it. Choose your guide wisely.

Badaling Highlights

Closest to Beijing

Just 60km from central Beijing. Your VIPChinaGuide escort handles all transport from your hotel — no subway transfers or tour bus crowds.

Wheelchair Accessible

Badaling is the only fully wheelchair-accessible Great Wall section, with lifts and ramps. VIPChinaGuide arranges accessible tours for travelers with reduced mobility.

Cable Car & Toboggan

Take the cable car up to the most spectacular ridge, then ride the toboggan back down — a thrilling family experience.

Year-Round Access

Open every day including winter, when snow-covered watchtowers create stunning photo opportunities your guide will capture for you.

Practical Information

  • Opening Hours: 7:00 AM – 6:00 PM (summer); 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM (winter)
  • Crowds: Most crowded of all Great Wall sections. Best visited weekday mornings or in winter.
  • Best Seasons: April–October for scenery; December–February for dramatic winter views
  • Cable Car: North cable car ¥100 one-way, ¥180 round-trip
  • Combined Ticket: Badaling + Ming Tombs day tour available — ask your guide
