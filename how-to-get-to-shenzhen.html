How to Get to Shenzhen ✈️

How to get to Shenzhen — metro (12 lines), taxi, DiDi, Shenzhen North HSR station, cross-border to Hong Kong, long-distance bus, and how to buy train tickets. Complete guide.

🚆 Shenzhen Transport Options

🚇

Shenzhen Metro — 12 Lines

Get a 深圳通 card (CNY 50 deposit) or WeChat/Alipay QR. Official app: 深圳地铁. Key: Line 1 (Luobao Road), Line 4 (Futian), Line 11 (Airport).

Shenzhen Taxi Fares

Starts CNY 10 for 2 km, CNY 2.6/km. Night surcharge 30% (11pm-6am). All metered.

DiDi Ride-Hailing (滴滴出行)

Shenzhen Train Stations

Shenzhen North (深圳北) — HSR to Guangzhou (35min), Hong Kong (18min), Beijing (9h), Shanghai (8h). Shenzhen Station (深圳站) — classic trains.
