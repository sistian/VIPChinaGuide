⚽ Chengdu Sports Events Guide

Major sporting events in Chengdu - from world-class marathons to international tournaments. Complete guide with past results and future outlook.

Top Sports Events in Chengdu

🏃

Chengdu Marathon (成都马拉松)

IAAF Gold Label certified marathon usually in October. The course passes Tianfu Square, Jinjiang River, and ancient Shu cultural sites. Growing international participation. Known for enthusiastic crowds and excellent race organization.

Full name: Chengdu Marathon (成都马拉松)

⚽

Chengdu Rongcheng FC - Chinese Super League

Chengdu's Chinese Super League team playing at Phoenix Sports Stadium. The team achieved promotion to the top tier in 2022. Season runs March-November with passionate local fan support.

Full name: Chengdu Rongcheng FC (成都蓉城)

🥋

Wudang Mountain International Martial Arts Festival

Major international martial arts event each autumn near Chengdu. Taoist kung fu masters, wushu athletes, and martial arts enthusiasts compete and train together. Combines competition with Taoist cultural immersion.

Full name: Wudang Mountain International Martial Arts Festival (武当山国际武术节)

🎾

Chengdu Open International Tennis Tournament

ATP Challenger tournament at Sichuan International Tennis Centre. Usually September-October. Emerging players and established professionals compete. Prize money and ranking points attract international field.

Full name: Chengdu Open (成都公开赛)

🏊

Chengdu Open Water Lake Race

Open water swimming event at Jinjiang River or reservoir. Swimmers compete with views of Chengdu's modern skyline and western mountains backdrop.

Full name: Chengdu Open Water Swimming Championship

🏃

Chengdu Universiade Legacy Sports Events

Following the successful 2021 Summer Universiade, Chengdu continues hosting major sports events at world-class venues built for the games. Swimming, athletics, and team sports venues still in active use.

Full name: Chengdu Summer Universiade (世界大学生夏季运动会) Legacy Events

Past Results and Future Outlook

Looking back at recent years and what to expect in 2026-2027

2024-2025

Recent Highlights

Chengdu Marathon 2025 was the largest edition yet with 35,000 runners. Chengdu Rongcheng FC finished in the top 4 of Chinese Super League 2024-2025 season. The 2025 Wudang Mountain International Martial Arts Festival attracted participants from 45 countries.

2026

2026 Season Outlook

Chengdu Marathon 2026 is expected to attract over 40,000 runners including increased international participation. Rongcheng FC continues building its squad for the 2026 CSL season. The annual Wudang Mountain martial arts event will expand its competition categories.

2027

2027 and Beyond

Chengdu is positioning itself as China's sports events hub for western China. New international events are being planned for the post-Universiade venue infrastructure. The Wudang Mountain area is being developed as a year-round martial arts training and competition destination.
