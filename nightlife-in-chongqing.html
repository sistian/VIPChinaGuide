🌙 Nightlife in Chongqing

Chongqing at night is spectacular — glowing skyscrapers above two rivers, late-night hot pot restaurants, and the world-class cable car over the Yangtze at night.

🔥 Top Nightlife Picks in Chongqing

🌃

Jiefangbei CBD Night View

Chongqing central business district spectacular at night — neon-lit streets of Jiefangbei, one of China most vibrant nighttime commercial districts.

🍲

Hualongqiao Night Food Street

Newest popular food street — hundreds of vendors serving hot pot, noodles, skewers and cold beer until 2am.

🌉

Hongyadong Night View

Iconic stilted building complex on cliff above Jialing River dramatically lit at night — symbol of Chongqing nightlife.

🍺

Yangtze River Cruise at Night

Night cruise on Yangtze or Jialing River passes under illuminated bridges — one of China most beautiful urban night views.

🌶️

Late-Night Hot Pot on Nanbin Road

Nanbin Road south bank outdoor hot pot restaurants where locals eat until 3am with river views.

🎵

Guanyinqiao Bar District (观音桥)

Most popular bar and club area — pedestrian street with hundreds of bars, KTV and late-night entertainment.
