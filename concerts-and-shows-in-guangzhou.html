🎭 Concerts and Shows in Guangzhou

Guangzhou's proximity to Hong Kong brings world-class international performances to the city — along with a strong tradition of Cantonese opera and a growing indie music scene.

🎵 Top Performances in Guangzhou

🎭

Cantonese Opera (粤剧) Performances

Traditional Cantonese Opera — a UNESCO intangible cultural heritage — performed at the Guangzhou Opera House and the Red Pearl Cantonese Opera Theatre. Colorful costumes, elaborate face painting, and distinctive singing.

🎻

Guangzhou Opera House Performances

Guangzhou's premier performing arts venue — the striking Zaha Hadid-designed building hosts opera, ballet, symphony concerts, and international touring productions.

🎸

Canton Music Festival (广东音乐节)

Held annually — classical music, jazz, and world music performances across Guangzhou's concert halls and outdoor venues. Usually in November.

🏟️

Guangzhou Olympic Sports Centre Concerts

Major arena concerts — international pop and rock artists, K-pop groups, and Chinese A-list performers. The largest venue in southern China for arena shows.

🎤

The Window Livehouse Indie Concerts

Guangzhou's best indie music venue — live shows by local and touring indie bands, rock acts, and electronic artists. Cover charges from CNY 80-200.

🎭

Guangzhou People's Art Theatre Productions

Drama, contemporary dance, and experimental theater productions. One of China's most respected regional theater companies.
