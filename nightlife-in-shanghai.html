🌙 Nightlife in Shanghai

Shanghai's nightlife is legendary — from Art Deco cocktail bars to 24-hour noodle shops. Here's where the city comes alive after dark.

🔥 Top Nightlife & Late-Night Food Picks

🌃

The Bund at Night

The iconic waterfront promenade with colonial buildings lit up in neon. Walk from the Peace Hotel to the Waigaoqiao building — Shanghai's most romantic evening stroll.

🍜

Nanjing Road Night Walk

Pedestrian shopping street transformed at night with LED screens and crowds. Connects People's Square to the Bund with endless food options.

🍺

Former French Concession Bars

The tree-lined streets of the former French Concession are packed with boutique cocktail bars, wine cellars, and live music venues in beautifully restored shikumen townhouses.

🥟

24-Hour Paradise for Late-Night Eaters

Shanghai never sleeps — 24-hour soup dumpling shops, midnight noodles at Jiaotong University area, and the Wujiang Road food street that comes alive at 11pm.

🌆

Xintiandi Nightlife

The renovated shikumen district with upscale bars, cocktail lounges, and restaurants — stylish evening destination with a preserved historical architecture backdrop.

🎵

JZ Club & Live Jazz Venues

Shanghai's legendary jazz scene — JZ Club, House of Blues & Jazz, and the rock venues on Dongping Road. Live music every night of the week.
