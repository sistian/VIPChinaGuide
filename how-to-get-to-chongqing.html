Chongqing Metro — 10+ Lines

hongqing Metro has 10+ lines including Line 2 — the world's most dramatic, running through residential towers and over flyovers. Official app: 重庆轨道交通. Due to 3D geography, metro stations span multiple levels.

Chongqing Taxi Fares

Starts CNY 10 for 3 km, CNY 2/km thereafter. Trust your driver on route — shortest distance is rarely fastest in this vertical city. All metered.

DiDi Ride-Hailing (滴滴出行)

Primary and often more reliable than taxis in this complex geography. Use for Nanshan, Ciqikou, and airport trips.

Chongqing Train Stations

Chongqing North (重庆北) — main HSR hub. Chongqing (重庆站) — classic in city center. Chongqing West (重庆西) — new hub. WARNING: North Station has 2 plazas 1.5 km apart.
