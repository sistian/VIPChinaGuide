Sports Events in Shiyan

Major sporting events in Shiyan - from world-class marathons to international tournaments. Complete guide with past results and future outlook.

Top Sports Events in Shiyan

🥋

Wudang Mountain International Kung Fu Marathon

Unique event combining Wudang kung fu training with marathon running. Participants train with Taoist masters before running through the Wudang Mountain scenic area. Cultural and sporting experience combining martial arts with endurance running. Usually October.

Full name: Wudang Kung Fu Marathon (武当功夫马拉松)

🏃

Shiyan Marathon (十堰马拉松)

City marathon in Shiyan. Course passes through the city and connects to Danjiangkou reservoir area. Growing local event attracting runners from across Hubei province. Usually October.

Full name: Shiyan Marathon (十堰马拉松)

🥋

Wudang Mountain Wushu Competition

Annual traditional wushu competition at Wudang Mountains. Taoist martial arts masters and wushu athletes compete in various categories. Held at Wudang Cultural Centre. Usually September.

Full name: Wudang Mountain International Wushu Competition (武当山国际武术比赛)

🏔️

Wudang Mountain Trail Marathon

Trail running through the Wudang Mountain scenic area. Course passes ancient Taoist temples, mountain peaks, and forest trails. Combines running with Taoist cultural sites. Usually October.

Full name: Wudang Mountain Trail Marathon (武当山越野马拉松)

🚣

Danjiangkou Reservoir Water Sports

Water sports events at the Danjiangkou reservoir near Shiyan. Rowing, dragon boat racing, and open water swimming. Usually summer months. The reservoir is China's largest artificial lake.

Full name: Danjiangkou Open Water Sports (丹江口水上运动)

🏃

Shiyan University Athletics Open

University athletics competition open to visitors. Track and field events at Shiyan University facilities. Regional university athletes compete. Usually May.

Full name: Shiyan University Athletics Open (十堰大学运动会)

Past Results and Future Outlook

Looking back at recent years and what to expect in 2026-2027

2024-2026

Recent Highlights

Wudang Kung Fu Marathon 2026 was the largest edition with 3,000 participants from 25 countries. The Danjiangkou dragon boat races attracted 50 teams in 2026. Wudang Wushu Competition hosted masters from 15 countries.

2026

2026 Season Outlook

The Wudang Kung Fu Marathon is expanding with a new half marathon category. New Taoist martial arts training camps are being offered year-round. The Shiyan Marathon is targeting provincial certification upgrade.

2027

2027 and Beyond

Shiyan is developing the Wudang Mountain area as a year-round martial arts and sports destination. New international martial arts schools are being established. The Danjiangkou area is being developed as a water sports training center.
