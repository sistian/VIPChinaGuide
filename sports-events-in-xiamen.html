Top Sports Events in Xiamen

🏃

Xiamen International Marathon (厦门马拉松)

AAF Gold Label certified marathon in January. One of China's most prestigious marathons - flat coastal course along the island ring road. Known for ideal running conditions and excellent organization. One of Asia's fastest courses.

Full name: Xiamen International Marathon (厦门马拉松)

🚴

Gulangyu Cycling Race

Amateur cycling race around Xiamen's Gulangyu Island. Flat scenic course on the car-free heritage island. International participants take the ferry to compete. Usually November.

Full name: Gulangyu Cycling Race (鼓浪屿自行车赛)

🏊

Xiamen Marathon Coastal Open Water Swim

Open water swimming event in the Xiamen Strait. Swim with views of Taiwan across the strait. Usually held in conjunction with the Xiamen Marathon weekend. Usually January.

Full name: Xiamen Strait Open Water Swim (厦门海峡横渡)

⚽

Xiamen Siming FC - China League One

China League One football at Xiamen Egret Stadium. The club represents Xiamen in the Chinese football pyramid. Season April to October.

Full name: Xiamen Siming FC (厦门鹭岛FC)

🏃

Xiamen University Marathon

University marathon at Xiamen University's lakeside campus. Scenic route through the campus and coastal Xiamen areas. Open to external participants.

Full name: Xiamen University Marathon (厦门大学马拉松)

🚣

Xiamen International Sailing Regatta

International sailing competition in Xiamen waters. The Xiamen Strait offers consistent winds for competitive sailing. Usually October-November. Attracts sailing teams from across Asia-Pacific.

Full name: Xiamen International Sailing Regatta (厦门国际帆船赛)

Past Results and Future Outlook

Looking back at recent years and what to expect in 2026-2027

2024-2026

Recent Highlights

Xiamen Marathon 2026 was the largest edition with 35,000 runners. The event maintained its World Athletics Platinum Label status. Xiamen Siming FC achieved promotion to China League One for the 2026 season.

2026

2026 Season Outlook

Xiamen Marathon 2026 will continue as one of Asia's premier January marathons with increased international elite participation. The Xiamen sailing regatta is expanding with more international sailing teams confirmed.

2027

2027 and Beyond

Xiamen is developing new water sports venues in the Xiang'an district. The city is in discussions to host an Asian Beach Games event. The Gulangyu cycling event is being expanded to a multi-day festival.
