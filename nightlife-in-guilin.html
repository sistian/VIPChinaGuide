🌙 Nightlife in Guilin

uilin nightlife is laid-back — scenic lakeside evening walks, local food streets and growing bar scene around Two Rivers and Four Lakes.

🔥 Top Nightlife Picks in Guilin

🌉

Two Rivers and Four Lakes Night Walk

ost romantic evening walk — illuminated pagodas, bridges and lakes reflected in water. Free and beautiful.

🍜

Zhengyang Road Night Food Street

Main night food street in Guilin city center — cheap and delicious rice noodles until 11pm.

🍺

Shahu Bar Area

Main bar district — small cluster of bars and cafes around Sanding Lake with outdoor seating in summer.

🌙

Elephant Trunk Hill Night View

Iconic limestone arch beautifully lit at night — best views from Nanxi Bridge and riverside promenade.

🍲

Yangshuo West Street Evening (西街)

Yangshuo legendary party street — bars, restaurants and street performers every evening.

🎵

Guilin Solo Cafe and Music

Small but growing indie music scene — local bands and open mic nights. Ask your guide for tonight schedule.
