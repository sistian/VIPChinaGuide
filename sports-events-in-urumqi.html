Sports Events in Urumqi

Major sporting events in Urumqi - from world-class marathons to international tournaments. Complete guide with past results and future outlook.

Top Sports Events in Urumqi

⛷️

Urumqi International Ski Championships

Major international skiing events at Silk Road Ski Resort and other facilities around Urumqi. World Cup level competitions in downhill, cross-country, and freestyle. Usually January-March. Elite international skiers compete.

Full name: Urumqi International Ski Championships (乌鲁木齐国际滑雪锦标赛)

🏃

Urumqi International Marathon (乌鲁木齐马拉松)

High-altitude marathon in Xinjiang's capital. Course passes through the city and nearby desert landscapes. Growing event attracting runners from across Central Asia. Usually September.

Full name: Urumqi International Marathon (乌鲁木齐马拉松)

🏔️

Tianshan Mountain Trail Running

Ultra trail running in the Tianshan Mountain range near Urumqi. Dramatic alpine scenery, glacier views, and high-altitude terrain. Attracts trail runners from across Asia. Multiple distance options.

Full name: Tianshan Mountain Trail Running (天山越野跑)

⚽

Xinjiang FA - China League Two

China League Two football representing the Xinjiang Uyghur Autonomous Region. Home matches at Urumqi Sports Stadium. Season May to October.

Full name: Xinjiang Football Association Team (新疆天山雪豹FC)

🏃

Taklamakan Desert Marathon

Marathon event on the edge of the Taklamakan Desert - one of world's largest deserts. Unique desert running experience. Challenging conditions with extreme temperatures. Usually October.

Full name: Taklamakan Desert Marathon (塔克拉玛干沙漠马拉松)

⛷️

Xinjiang Winter Sports Games

Regional winter sports games featuring skiing, snowboarding, and ice sports. Teams from across Xinjiang compete in multiple winter sports disciplines. Usually February-March.

Full name: Xinjiang Winter Sports Games (新疆冬季运动会)

Past Results and Future Outlook

Looking back at recent years and what to expect in 2026-2027

2024-2026

Recent Highlights

Urumqi International Ski Championships 2026 hosted World Cup level events for the first time. The Urumqi Marathon had record participation in 2026 with runners from 20 countries. Xinjiang FA team had a competitive season in China League Two.

2026

2026 Season Outlook

Urumqi is expanding its ski resort infrastructure with new facilities at the Silk Road resort. The marathon continues to grow as a major Central Asian running event. New desert ultra events are being developed for the Taklamakan edge.

2027

2027 and Beyond

Urumqi is positioning itself as China's winter sports hub for Central Asia. New international winter sports events are being planned including potential World Cup ski events. The Tianshan trail running event is seeking UTMB qualification.
