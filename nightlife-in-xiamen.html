🌙 Nightlife in Xiamen

Xiamen tropical coastal location shapes its nightlife — seaside bars, vibrant night markets and food culture that runs late.

🔥 Top Nightlife Picks in Xiamen

🎹

Gulangyu Island Evening Walk

Take last ferry to Gulangyu — colonial island enchanting at night with street piano music drifting from cafes.

🍜

Zengcuoan Night Market (曾厝垵)

Most colorful night market — hundreds of food stalls, local snacks in former fishing village. Open until midnight.

🌊

Xiamen Coastal Night Walk

31-km island ring road and coastal promenade beautiful at night — Taiwan Strait shimmers with passing ship lights.

🍺

Huli Hub Bar District (湖里区)

Main bar and club area — local craft beer bars, cocktail lounges and clubs near the coastal area.

🦐

Zengcuoan Seafood Late Night

Area around Zengcuoan has seafood restaurants open until 11pm — fresh catch from Taiwan Strait.

🎵

Xiamen Coffee and Live Music Culture

Well-established independent coffee culture — live acoustic venues and open-mic nights popular.
